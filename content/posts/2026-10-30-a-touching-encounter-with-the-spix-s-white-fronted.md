On the first day of sailing the Rio Negro, as part of the Tropical Field Mycology course led by João Araújo, I had a brief and meaningful connection with this small primate. Although the experience soaked me in child-like fascination, it was made clear to me that the reason they were so comfortable around humans is habituation. These small friends are being fed intentionally to help attract tourists to the area.

Knowledge like that leaves one with a bitter taste. There is overwhelming consensus in the scientific community that feeding wild animals causes more harm than good [sauce].
