This is an example page, created so you can see how new pages look and work. Feel free to edit or delete it from the admin panel — it won't affect anything else on the site.

## How this works

Any page you add here shows up automatically in the site's navigation menu, and is reachable at its own address once it's live.

You can use:

- **Bold** and *italic* text
- [Links](https://ndrshfflr.github.io/)
- Simple lists like this one

### A smaller heading

Paragraphs, headings, and lists are all you need for most pages — a tour description, a short write-up, or a simple announcement.
