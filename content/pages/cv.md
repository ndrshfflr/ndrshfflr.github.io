**Creative and driven biologist & nature enthusiast**

I am a Master's student in microbiology, focusing mainly on fungi. In my spare time, I explore their potential in creating more sustainable food systems, whereas my research is focused on using molecular methods to discover hidden mould in buildings. Apart from that, I love taking pictures and getting lost in deep thought on the nature of reality.
