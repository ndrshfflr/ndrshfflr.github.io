Data. Spore dimensions, Q-values and diagnoses marked Svampeatlas are taken from the species descriptions on Danmarks Svampeatlas (Foreningen til Svampekundskabens Fremme). Records marked literature are from standard works — chiefly Funga Nordica (Knudsen & Vesterholt) and Assyov's Boletes of Europe — used where Svampeatlas carries no measurement. Every species card names its source.
Distribution. Occurrence counts are fetched live from the GBIF API, which aggregates the national portals: Svampeatlas (DK), Artsobservasjoner/Artskart (NO), Artportalen (SE), laji.fi (FI), and records from Iceland, Greenland and the Faroes. Counts are observation records, not abundance.
Photographs. Field photos attached to GBIF occurrence records, fetched live and filtered to Creative Commons and public-domain licences only — each image's own licence is checked, because GBIF's record-level licence field is frequently more restrictive than the photo's and filtering on it server-side discards most usable images. All-rights-reserved material (much of the Norwegian Artsobservasjoner library) is never displayed. Photographer, licence and country are shown on every thumbnail and each links to its source record. These are recorder determinations, not verified identifications.
Spore micro-characters. Ornamentation, apex, wall, Melzer's reaction, cyanophily, guttulation and symmetry. Boletales are conservative in these characters, so where a species has no separately published value it inherits its family's typical state — marked with ° throughout, and never presented as if it had been checked for that species. Unmarked values are documented for the species itself, usually quoted from Svampeatlas.
Nomenclature follows current generic concepts (Boletus s.str. split into Suillellus, Neoboletus, Rubroboletus, Caloboletus, Butyriboletus, Imperator, Cyanoboletus, Hortiboletus, Xerocomellus, Imleria, Hemileccinum); Svampeatlas names are shown as synonyms where they differ.
