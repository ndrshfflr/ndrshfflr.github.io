About

Science first. Curiosity always.

I hold an MSc in Biology and specialise in mycology — the study of fungi. My work sits at the intersection of careful laboratory practice and a lifelong fascination with the roles fungi play, above and below ground.

Professionally, I work as an indoor microbiology specialist: sampling buildings, analysing what I find in the lab, and helping people understand and remediate what's growing where it shouldn't. Outside of that, mycology is also where I spend my free time — cultivating mushrooms, foraging, and following fungi into stranger territory: the guided tours, workshops, and integrative work I'm building toward now.

Services

Indoor Microbiology

For homeowners, landlords, and businesses dealing with mould, damp, or unexplained air quality issues. I bring lab-grade rigour to a problem that's usually diagnosed by guesswork.

01 · On-site

Sampling

Careful, methodical collection of air and surface samples to identify what's actually present — not just what's visible.

02 · Laboratory

Analysis & Interpretation

Microscopy and lab analysis of samples, translated into a clear, plain-language read of what's growing and why.

03 · Resolution

Remediation Guidance

Practical, evidence-based recommendations for resolving the underlying issue — not just masking the symptoms.

Beyond the lab

Where biology meets the unseen

Alongside my clinical and field work, I'm drawn to the wider questions fungi raise — about consciousness, meaning, and how humans relate to the living world. This is the territory I'm slowly building future work toward: the intersection of biology with Jungian psychology, animism, esotericism, mysticism, and compassion-based approaches to healing, including the study of psychedelics.

This isn't a formal service yet — it's an ongoing personal and intellectual pursuit, and the direction I'd like my work to grow into. If this resonates with you, or you want to talk about it, I'm always happy to hear from people thinking along similar lines.
